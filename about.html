Our Story

A Journey Through Art and Culture

Sunita Arts is a premier space for Indian Art and an Online Art Gallery presenting the most enchanting Indian Art Paintings and Sculptures in Contai. Our collection includes creations by some of the best emerging and renowned artists of India.

We serve as a platform for young and established artists to showcase their artwork on a global stage, currently boasting one of the widest collections of contemporary paintings, drawings, folk art, sculptures, and photographs.

Mission & Vision

Our commitment to art and culture

Our Mission

To promote and preserve Indian art and culture by providing a platform for artists to showcase their work, connect with art enthusiasts, and contribute to the global art community. We strive to make art accessible to everyone while maintaining the highest standards of quality and authenticity.

Our Vision

To become the leading destination for Indian contemporary and traditional art, fostering creativity and cultural exchange. We envision a world where art bridges communities, inspires innovation, and preserves our rich cultural heritage for future generations.

Our Values

What drives us every day

Passion

We are passionate about art and committed to sharing that passion with the world.

Integrity

We maintain the highest standards of authenticity and ethical practices in all our dealings.

Innovation

We embrace new technologies and methods to make art more accessible and engaging.

Community

We believe in building strong relationships within the art community and beyond.
